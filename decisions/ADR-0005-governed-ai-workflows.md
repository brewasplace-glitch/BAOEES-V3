# ADR-0005 — Governed AI workflows

## Status

Accepted.

## Decision

Phoenix AI workflows operate under explicit policy and produce traceable
decision records. They may not silently invent project facts.

## Consequences

- Assumptions require a source and confidence.
- Failures remain visible and auditable.
- Autonomous execution can expand without removing governance.
- BB12–BB14 integrations can be orchestrated through declared capabilities.
