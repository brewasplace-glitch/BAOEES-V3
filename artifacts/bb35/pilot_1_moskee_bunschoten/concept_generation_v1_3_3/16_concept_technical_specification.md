# Concept Technical Specification

**CONCEPT - NOT FOR EXECUTION OR SUBMISSION**

Project: Uitbreiding Haci Bayram Moskee
Address: Bikkersweg 88, 3752 WV Bunschoten-Spakenburg

## 1. Scope
Two-storey extension measuring 7.00 x 10.00 m with approximately 140 m2 gross floor area.

## 2. General quality rule
No part of this concept may be used for construction, procurement, permit submission or structural sizing.

## 3. Concept materials
- primary_structure: Steel frame - grade/profile unresolved (columns and beams).
- floor: Composite or precast floor - unresolved (upper floor).
- roof: Lightweight insulated low-slope roof (roof).
- facade: Insulated masonry or rainscreen concept (external walls).
- glazing: Thermally insulated glazing concept (windows and entrance).
- internal_walls: Lightweight partitions / masonry mix (room separation).
- wet_rooms: Water-resistant finishes and drainage (ablution and toilets).
- fire: Fire-rated materials subject to fire strategy (escape and compartmentation).
- foundation: Reinforced concrete concept only (foundations and ground beams).

## 4. Required professional verification
- Concept floor-to-floor height is 3.30 m.
- Concept roof is a low-slope roof behind a 0.60 m parapet.
- Preliminary structural option is a steel frame on a 3 x 3 column grid.
- Floor system is provisionally a lightweight composite or precast floor.
- Foundation type is unresolved pending structural survey and geotechnical evidence.
- No load is transferred to the existing building until the connection is professionally verified.
- Fire resistance, compartmentation and escape routes require Bbl/fire-safety assessment.
- Occupancy and prayer-place capacity remain external verified inputs.
- Available parking information is preliminary and not field-verified.
- No final AERIUS result is generated without verified activity and traffic data.
- Material selections are concept placeholders and may not be procured.
- Cost band uses illustrative planning allowances, not market-verified quotations.
- Site plan is schematic until the original cadastral DWG or survey base is supplied.
- Room boundaries are reconstructed conceptually from available overview plans.

## 5. Execution hold point
Release for execution requires a signed technical design, calculations, specifications, drawings, permit evidence and an approved BB35 pilot acceptance record.
