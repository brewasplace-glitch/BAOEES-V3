# PEK Loads Wave 1 v1.0

Implements the first 30 Loads functions in the Engineering Kernel registry.

Capabilities include:

- generic load records and normalized directions;
- dead, imposed, wind, snow, seismic and thermal loads;
- hydrostatic and earth-pressure calculations;
- line-load, area-load and point-load transformations;
- uniform, triangular and trapezoidal resultants;
- moments and vector components;
- load scaling and vector summation;
- resultant loads;
- scalar and vector load combinations;
- characteristic, design and accidental combinations;
- dynamic amplification.

No regulatory factors are hard-coded. Partial factors, combination factors and
national annex values must be supplied by future standards layers.
