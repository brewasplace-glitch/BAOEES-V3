# PEK-10 Foundation & Geotechnical Design Engine — Wave 1 v1.0

This domain extension introduces 30 generic SI-based foundation functions.

Coverage:

- rectangular foundation area, volume and self-weight;
- average and net contact pressure;
- eccentricity, kern limit and kern validation;
- rectangular base pressure distribution;
- effective foundation width;
- allowable vertical load, utilization and bearing safety;
- sliding and overturning checks;
- one-dimensional, total and differential settlement;
- angular distortion and settlement utilization;
- strip-footing line load and required width;
- simple foundation-beam reactions, moment and shear;
- pile-group capacity, average pile load and utilization;
- equal support-load distribution and load-uniformity assessment.

The module deliberately does not embed national design factors or project-specific
soil parameters. Those remain controlled through Phoenix project configuration.
