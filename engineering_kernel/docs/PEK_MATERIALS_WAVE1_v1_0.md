# PEK Materials Wave 1 v1.0

Implements the first 30 Materials functions in the Engineering Kernel registry.

Capabilities include:

- generic and domain-specific material records;
- concrete, structural steel, reinforcement steel, timber and masonry;
- aluminium, glass, plastics and soil;
- shear modulus, bulk modulus and specific weight;
- stress, strain and linear elastic transformations;
- characteristic and design values;
- safety factors and utilization;
- temperature and moisture factors;
- thermal strain and thermal expansion;
- creep-adjusted modulus and shrinkage deformation;
- classification, validation and immutable material adjustment.

The module does not hard-code regulatory design classes. Code-specific material
tables and national annex values belong in future standards/data layers.
