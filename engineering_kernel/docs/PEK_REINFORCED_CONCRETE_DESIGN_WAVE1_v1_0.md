# PEK-11 Reinforced Concrete Design Engine — Wave 1 v1.0

This domain extension introduces 30 generic SI-based reinforced-concrete
design functions.

Coverage:

- concrete design strength, tensile strength and elastic modulus;
- concrete unit weight and section self-weight;
- reinforcement design strength, bar area and reinforcement ratio;
- effective depth, lever arm and required tension reinforcement;
- minimum and maximum reinforcement;
- flexural utilization;
- concrete shear stress and shear capacity;
- required shear reinforcement and stirrup spacing;
- shear utilization;
- concrete, steel and combined axial column capacity;
- column slenderness and axial-moment interaction index;
- crack-control ratio and deflection utilization;
- nominal cover, anchorage length and development length;
- serviceability pass/fail evaluation.

The module intentionally does not embed a national design code. Partial factors,
detailing limits, durability classes and national annexes remain configurable
through Phoenix project and policy layers.
