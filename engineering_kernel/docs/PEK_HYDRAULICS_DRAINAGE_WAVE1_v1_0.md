# PEK-09 Hydraulics & Drainage Engine — Wave 1 v1.0

This domain extension introduces 30 generic SI-based functions for:

- rainfall depth, rainfall volume and runoff volume;
- Rational Method peak flow;
- weighted runoff coefficients;
- circular and rectangular flow geometry;
- hydraulic radius;
- Manning and Chezy velocity/discharge;
- full-pipe discharge;
- flow velocity and travel time;
- detention storage and detention time;
- infiltration volume and required infiltration area;
- Darcy flow;
- pump sizing and outlet counts;
- water balance and continuity error;
- pipe fill ratio;
- drainage-specific discharge;
- roof outlet flow;
- required pipe area and equivalent diameter.

Project-specific climate datasets, local authority rules and design standards
remain external configuration inputs.
