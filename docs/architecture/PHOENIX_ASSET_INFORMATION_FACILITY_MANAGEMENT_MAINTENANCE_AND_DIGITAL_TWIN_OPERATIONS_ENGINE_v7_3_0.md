# Phoenix Asset Information, Facility Management, Maintenance and Digital Twin Operations Engine v7.3.0

v7.3.0 extends Phoenix from handover into operational asset management.

Core capabilities:
- asset register;
- O&M document register;
- warranty register;
- preventive maintenance register;
- inspection register;
- fault register;
- service history;
- spare parts;
- replacement cycles;
- operations readiness matrix;
- operational Digital Twin writeback;
- SHA-256 artifact manifest.

Automatic operations release is disabled. Operations-ready unlock requires the
upstream handover gate, complete mandatory asset data, verified O&M documents,
verified warranties, complete maintenance plans, passed initial inspections,
critical spare-part coverage where applicable, and professional operations
acceptance.
