# BB17.2 — Multi-Jurisdiction Building Codepack Foundation v1.0.0

BB17.2 establishes separate legal foundations for European Netherlands,
Suriname, Caribbean Netherlands (Bonaire, Sint Eustatius and Saba), Aruba,
Curaçao and Sint Maarten.

Phoenix selects exactly one primary legal jurisdiction from project
location data. It never silently combines legal codepacks from different
jurisdictions. Non-legal engineering overlays may be stacked for climate,
water, corrosion, biological durability, extreme wind, seismic exposure
and geotechnical conditions.

All six bundled legal profiles are intentionally empty, draft and inactive.
They identify official source locations but make no legal-compliance claim.
Article-level rules, effective dates, local implementation rules, numerical
hazard parameters and independent review must be added before activation.
