Wave 12 consumes verified Wave 9 axial-force results and performs transparent
axial resistance checks for timber and masonry members.

The engine reports compression and tension resistance, governing resistance,
utilization, optional geometric slenderness screening and explicit pass/fail
evidence.

The implementation is generic and policy-based. It does not claim compliance
with a named design standard.

The slenderness calculation is only a screen. Timber buckling, connection
design, load-duration and moisture models, masonry eccentricity, lateral
stability, unit-mortar interaction, fire and durability remain outside the
verified scope and require separate engines and competent engineer review.
