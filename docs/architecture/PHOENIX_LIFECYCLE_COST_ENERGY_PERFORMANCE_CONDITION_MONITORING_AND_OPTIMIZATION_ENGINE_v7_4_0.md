# Phoenix Lifecycle Cost, Energy, Performance, Condition Monitoring and Optimization Engine v7.4.0

v7.4.0 extends Phoenix from operational asset management into lifecycle
performance analysis and controlled optimization.

Core capabilities:
- lifecycle cost register and present-value summary;
- energy and resource performance registers;
- KPI and performance-deviation registers;
- condition monitoring;
- maintenance and replacement forecasts;
- optimization recommendation register;
- lifecycle performance/optimization matrix;
- Digital Twin lifecycle optimization writeback;
- SHA-256 artifact manifest.

Automatic optimization action is disabled. Optimization-action unlock requires
the upstream operations gate, verified financial assumptions, reliable energy,
condition, KPI and lifecycle-cost data, evidence-backed optimization
recommendations and professional approval.
