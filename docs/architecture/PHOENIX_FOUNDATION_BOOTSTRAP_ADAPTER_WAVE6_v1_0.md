Wave 6.1 is the corrected, syntax-validated rebuild of Wave 6.

The adapter verifies the SHA-256 of the geotechnical input artifact and creates
a preliminary foundation concept for strip, pad, raft, pile or undetermined
foundation types.

The optional Phoenix standard strip concept contains:

- continuous strip: 1.50 m wide and 0.40 m high;
- centered foundation beam: 0.50 m wide and 0.60 m high.

These dimensions remain assumptions until project-specific loads, ground
investigation, bearing resistance, settlement, structural capacity,
reinforcement and constructability have been verified.

The installer can recover from the failed Wave 6 installation, but refuses to
continue when unrelated repository changes are present.
