# Project Phoenix Reinforced Concrete Beam Design Engine v1.0.0

This engine performs transparent preliminary design of a rectangular reinforced-concrete beam on two supports.

Capabilities:
- self-weight, distributed loads and point loads;
- ULS and SLS combinations;
- reactions, shear and bending moment distributions;
- preliminary longitudinal reinforcement and shear links;
- cracked-section deflection estimate;
- crack-width estimate;
- anchorage and support-bearing checks;
- XLSX, PDF, DOCX, SVG, DXF, HTML, JSON and CSV exports.

The default Dutch preliminary profile references the current NEN-EN 1992-1-1 publication and the National Annex including A2:2025. Users must hold the applicable licensed standards and obtain review and signature by a competent structural engineer. Fire design, robustness, seismic design, fatigue and accidental actions are not included in v1.0.0.
