# Phoenix Structural Engineering Suite
## v8.0.0 Architectural-to-Structural Model Derivation Engine

v8.0.0 is the first production block of the Phoenix Structural Engineering Suite.

It derives a traceable structural candidate model from the architectural and
detailed architectural models.

Generated candidate information:
- structural axes;
- loadbearing-wall candidates;
- column candidates;
- beam candidates;
- slab panels and candidate span directions;
- roof-support candidates;
- support/geometry relationships;
- stability zones;
- material hypotheses;
- source architectural element/space traceability.

This engine does **not** perform final load determination, structural analysis,
member design or professional structural approval. Those are intentionally
reserved for v8.1 through v8.8.

The structural release gate remains locked.
